Frequently asked

Is my progress saved if I get a new phone?
Progress is stored only on your device, so it won't transfer automatically between devices. Restoring your "Remove Ads" purchase, however, works on any device signed into the same Apple ID via Settings → Remove Ads → Restore Purchases.

How do I turn off ads?
Go to Settings → Remove Ads for a one-time, ad-free upgrade.

How do I stop reminder notifications?
Go to Settings → Notifications and turn off Reminders.
