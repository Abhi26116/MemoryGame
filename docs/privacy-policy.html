Privacy Policy

Memory Match · Last updated: July 2026

Memory Match ("the App") is designed to work fully offline. This policy explains what information is, and is not, collected when you use the App.

Summary

No account or login No data sold Progress stored only on your device No location access

Information We Do Not Collect

The App does not require you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, or any other personal information directly. Your game progress, stars, achievements, and settings are stored locally on your device and are never transmitted to us.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob — displays advertising within the App to support free gameplay. The App is configured to request advertising under Google's child-directed treatment setting, which limits ad personalization and behavioral tracking. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — manages advertising consent choices where required by local regulations.
  • Apple StoreKit — processes the optional one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase. Payment information is handled entirely by Apple; we do not receive or store your payment details. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications directly on your device using Apple's notification framework. No notification data is sent to any server, and you can disable reminders at any time in Settings.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising in the App is requested under Google's child-directed treatment setting, which disables personalized advertising and behavioral remarketing.

Data Retention & Deletion

All game data is stored locally on your device. You can erase it at any time using Settings → Data → Reset All Progress, or by deleting the App.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
